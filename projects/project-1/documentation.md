# Project 1 Documentation – Literacy Rate, Internet Usage, Life Expectancy, and GDP per Capita

## Data
The data used in this project can be found in the public/data folder. It depicts four datasets by country:
- Literacy rate | [Link to source](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/cross-country-literacy-rates)
- Share of individuals using the Internet | [Link to source](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/share-of-individuals-using-the-internet?country=WB_SA~WB_NA~WB_SSA~WB_EAP)
- Life expectancy | [Link to source](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/life-expectancy?country=OWID_WRL~Americas~OWID_EUR~OWID_AFR)
- GDP per capita | [Link to source](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/gdp-per-capita-maddison-project-database)

The data was obtained from the [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org) website and covers the most recent years the data was collected. 
The literacy rate depicts the percentage of people aged 15 and older who can read and write a simple sentence about their daily life. 
The internet usage data depicts the share of who used internet in the last three months. The life expectancy data depicts the number of years the average person born in a certain year would live if they experienced the same chances of dying at each age as people did that year. 
The GDP per capita depcits the gross domestic product divided by the population adjusted for inflation and differences in living costs between countries.
